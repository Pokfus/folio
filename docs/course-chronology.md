# Course chronology (Oct 2026)

The dates the **course collections** print — Politics: East Asia (`pea-`), The EU as an External Power (`eep-`)
and Gateway to Global Affairs (`gga-`) — so that every date line and every date in prose agrees across the three
and with the record. It is not a subject chronology: it holds treaties, summits, votes, laws, congresses and
dated events, with the source that fixes each. The rules are in `docs/course-refinement-audit.md`.

**Conventions**

- Years are CE and carry no era except under 1000. A day of the month is printed only where a source gives it.
- A date the course presents and the record corrects is given both ways, with whose it is (see the ledger's
  `SLIDE-VS-RECORD` lines in the audit file).
- The 2026 events (the Iran war, the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, the 2026 NATO summit) are dated as the
  supplied course text reports them, with a dated primary or news source; where only the course text reports
  one, the card says "according to".
- A generic government term with no pinned start has no row here: its date line is empty and the card is
  `undatable`.

**Pins.** One line per card whose date line must carry the figures after the colon (separated by `;`); read by
`greece-audit.js` (rule `D.pin`).
