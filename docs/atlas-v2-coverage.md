GENERATED by `.claude/atlas-build/build-history.js --install` (Phase 2b; the presence layer, Phase 2c) from `atlas/data/history.bin` — never edit by hand; rebuild instead.

The historical layer draws **the states Folio's cards teach** — the card-linked polity series of `.claude/polity-spec.json` that Cliopatria v0.2.0 carries, 157 of them — not every state of the period. **Peoples and cultures (Phase 2c)** — 34 series: eight Cliopatria peoples, the Cyclades and Crete as their islands, the Yangshao and Longshan as outlines of dated sites — are a presence drawn over the states (no border, a soft edge), so they are named in the last column and are NOT counted in the land share. The table reads each century at its first year (1 CE for the first century CE) and measures the land the alive faces cover against the land outside Antarctica in the core partition (134.6 million km²; Antarctica's 13.8 million km² left out). "Contested" is land two polities claim in that year; "nested" is a member drawn inside its overlord.

**Where the layer is thin.** Under 2 % of the land is mapped in 3400 BCE – 600 BCE, 2000 CE: the cards' states are few and small there, or Cliopatria has no row for them. The fullest century is 1800 CE (28.4 % of the land, 12 polities). Everywhere else the plain land is not "no state": it is a state no card teaches yet, or one Cliopatria does not draw.

## The 24 war sides that resolved to no entity (Phase 2c's inventory) and what Phase 2d did with each

The places registry's migration (`docs/atlas-places-report.md`) listed 24 war sides — an authored extent or a named side of a war card — that named no entity of this file. Phase 2d resolved each as far as an open source allows and **built nothing from a source that is not clearly open**: a side is **built** (a polity series answers it: Cliopatria's rows, or an assembly of series — membership by date over entities, never a new shape), **derived** (an extent made from a pinned open source: `.claude/atlas-build/war-extents.json`, hashes held against `sources.json`), **authored** (kept as the war card's own hand-drawn extent — an entity of kind `extent`, drawn as a soft, borderless wash in the side's tint and said on its card to be Folio's own approximation — and **deferred**, with the reason) or **unresolved** (named by the card's keys and drawn by nothing, said on the place card). The same table is held by `.claude/test-war-sides.js`.
