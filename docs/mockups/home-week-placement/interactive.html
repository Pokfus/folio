Where the Today / This week box goes on a tablet and a desktop

On a phone the figures box already sits below Your collections and above Minigames. Here are three ways to do the same on a tablet (T1–T3, an 834px frame) and on a desktop (D1–D3, a 1280px window with Folio's 800px column). The moved box carries a dashed “moved” marker you can turn off. Figures are samples: 40 cards, 16 min, 80% recalled today; 211 cards, 1 h 52 min, 84% this week. Answer with one of each, e.g. “T2 and D1”.
