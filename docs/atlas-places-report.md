## An island and the polis of the same name, one place (3c, decision 1)

Candidates of one toponym that are islands and settlements only, within 25 km, where the settlement side is a polis or an ancient city (a separate item, or the island's own item carrying the polis class), are ONE site: the island row survives (the toponym names the island) and the polis is its alias and a `merged` item.
